Foil
Type
Edition
Effect
+50 Chips

Foil is an edition card modifier that makes a Joker or playing card give +50 Chips.

Foil adds +$2 to the buy price of jokers and playing cards.

Jokers

[edit | edit source]
  • Foil Jokers can naturally appear in the Shop with a 2% chance.
    • The chance to get a Foil can be increased with ({{hl|edition|Foil}}, {{hl|edition|Holographic}}, and {{hl|edition|Polychrome}} cards appear {{hl|orange|2X}} more often Base Hone.png) Hone & ({{hl|edition|Foil}}, {{hl|edition|Holographic}}, and {{hl|edition|Polychrome}} cards appear {{hl|orange|4X}} more often Have at least 5 Joker cards with Foil, Holographic, or Polychrome (or Negative) effect Upgraded Glow Up.png) Glow Up by 2X and 4X respectively.
  • (Create a copy of a random [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]], destroy all other Jokers <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes {{hl|edition|Negative}} from copy)}} Available from start. Spectral Ankh.png) Ankh is able to duplicate Foil Jokers.
  • (After {{hl|orange|2}} rounds, sell this card to {{hl|orange|Duplicate}} a random Joker <br> {{currently|{{hl|orange|0}}/2}} <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] from copy)}} Win a run without ever having more than {{hl|orange|4 Jokers}} Rare Effect N/A (Passive) Invisible Joker.png) Invisible Joker can copy a Foil Joker, and the copy will keep the edition.
  • ({{hl|green|1 in 4}} chance to add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] edition to a random [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]] Available from start. Tarot The Wheel of Fortune.png) The Wheel of Fortune has a 50% chance of applying Foil to a random Joker if it activates.
  • ( Discover the {{edition|Foil}} edition Foil tag.png) Foil Tag applies Foil to the next base edition joker in the shop and makes it free.

Playing Cards

[edit | edit source]
  • Foil playing cards can be naturally acquired from Standard Packs with a 4% chance.
    • The chance to get a Foil can be increased with ({{hl|edition|Foil}}, {{hl|edition|Holographic}}, and {{hl|edition|Polychrome}} cards appear {{hl|orange|2X}} more often Base Hone.png) Hone & ({{hl|edition|Foil}}, {{hl|edition|Holographic}}, and {{hl|edition|Polychrome}} cards appear {{hl|orange|4X}} more often Have at least 5 Joker cards with Foil, Holographic, or Polychrome (or Negative) effect Upgraded Glow Up.png) Glow Up by 2X and 4X respectively.
  • Foil playing cards are able to be purchased from the Shop if ({{hl|orange|Playing cards}} in shop may have an [[Card modifiers#Enhancements|{{hl|sblue|Enhancement}}]], [[Card modifiers#Edition|{{hl|edition|Edition}}]], and/or a [[Card modifiers#Seal|{{hl|orange|Seal}}]] Buy a total of 20 Playing cards from the shop Upgraded Illusion.png) Illusion is owned.
  • (Add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] effect to {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in hand Available from start. Spectral Aura.png) Aura has a 50% chance to apply Foil to a selected playing card in your hand.
  • (Select {{hl|orange|2}} cards, convert the {{hl|orange|left}} card into the {{hl|orange|right}} card<br>{{hl|gray|(Drag to rearrange)}} Available from start. Tarot Death.png) Death, (Create {{hl|orange|2}} copies of {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in your hand Available from start. Spectral Cryptid.png) Cryptid, and (If {{hl|orange|first hand}} of round has only {{hl|orange|1}} card, add a permanent copy to deck and draw it to {{hl|orange|hand}} Rare Effect On Played DNA.png) DNA can duplicate Foil playing cards.

Synergies

[edit | edit source]

Jokers

[edit | edit source]
  • Due to being an edition that is often given to playing cards (through Standard Packs, (Add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] effect to {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in hand Available from start. Spectral Aura.png) Aura, etc.), Jokers that can retrigger playing cards that may have Foil like (Retrigger {{hl|orange|first}} played card used in scoring {{hl|orange|2}} additional times Beat a Boss Blind with a {{ph|High Card}} Common Retrigger On Scored Hanging Chad.png) Hanging Chad, (Retrigger all played {{hl|orange|face}} cards Play a total of {{hl|orange|300}} face cards Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Sock and Buskin.png) Sock and Buskin, and (Retrigger each played {{hl|orange|2}}, {{hl|orange|3}}, {{hl|orange|4}}, or {{hl|orange|5}} Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Hack.png) Hack have the ability to indirectly provide hundreds of Chips through retriggers.
  • Nearly all Jokers that give a large amount of Mult (namely, ones that are able to give a high amount of +Mult like (When [[Blinds|{{hl|orange|Blind}}]] is selected, destroy [[Jokers|Joker]] to the right and permanently add {{hl|orange|double}} its sell value to this {{hl|red|Mult}} <br> {{currently|{{mult|+0}}}} Uncommon Additive Mult Independent Ceremonial Dagger.png) Ceremonial Dagger or (This Joker gains {{Mult|+2}} per {{hl|orange|reroll}} in [[The Shop|the shop]] <br> {{Currently|{{mult|+0}}}} Uncommon Additive Mult Independent Flash Card.png) Flash Card) benefit from Chips increasing often much more significantly than Mult.
    • This is because if a hand is currently at, say, 10 Chips X 100 Mult, +50 Chips would increase the net score by a much higher amount than +50 Mult would.

  • ( {{Spectral|}} cards may appear in the shop, start with a {{spectral|Hex}} card Win a run with the {{D|Yellow}} on [[Stakes|any difficulty]] Ghost Deck.png) Ghost Deck makes it much easier to find spectral cards like (Add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] effect to {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in hand Available from start. Spectral Cryptid.png) Cryptid that are able to either make existing cards Foil or create new Foil cards.

Seals

[edit | edit source]
  • (Retrigger this card {{hl|orange|1}} time Seal Red Seal.png) Red Seals are able to be applied to Foil playing cards, causing each one to give +100 Chips.

Strategies

[edit | edit source]
  • Due to the fact that +50 Chips is a quite large amount of score, obtaining even just two Foil Jokers can often lead the player into not needing a Joker that provides Chips. This is especially the case if Foil playing cards are utilized, as playing cards can be retriggered and are much more easily able to be duplicated compared to Jokers.

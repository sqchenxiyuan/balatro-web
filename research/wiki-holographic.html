Holographic is an edition card modifier that makes a joker or a playing card give +10 Mult.

Holographic adds +$3 to the buy price of jokers and playing cards.

Acquisition

[edit | edit source]

Jokers

[edit | edit source]
  • Holographic Jokers can naturally appear in the Shop with a 1.4% chance.
    • The chance to get a Holographic can be increased with ({{hl|edition|Foil}}, {{hl|edition|Holographic}}, and {{hl|edition|Polychrome}} cards appear {{hl|orange|2X}} more often Base Hone.png) Hone & ({{hl|edition|Foil}}, {{hl|edition|Holographic}}, and {{hl|edition|Polychrome}} cards appear {{hl|orange|4X}} more often Have at least 5 Joker cards with Foil, Holographic, or Polychrome (or Negative) effect Upgraded Glow Up.png) Glow Up by 2X and 4X respectively.
  • ({{hl|green|1 in 4}} chance to add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] edition to a random [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]] Available from start. Tarot The Wheel of Fortune.png) The Wheel of Fortune has a 35% chance to add Holographic to a random Joker if it activates.
  • ( Discover the {{edition|Holographic}} edition Holographic tag.png) Holographic Tag applies Holographic to the next base edition joker in the shop and makes it free.
  • (Create a copy of a random [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]], destroy all other Jokers <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes {{hl|edition|Negative}} from copy)}} Available from start. Spectral Ankh.png) Ankh is able to duplicate Holographic Jokers.
  • (After {{hl|orange|2}} rounds, sell this card to {{hl|orange|Duplicate}} a random Joker <br> {{currently|{{hl|orange|0}}/2}} <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] from copy)}} Win a run without ever having more than {{hl|orange|4 Jokers}} Rare Effect N/A (Passive) Invisible Joker.png) Invisible Joker can copy a Holographic Joker, and the copy will keep the edition.

Playing Cards

[edit | edit source]
  • Holographic playing cards can be naturally acquired from Standard Packs with a 2.8% chance.
    • The chance to get a Holographic can be increased with ({{hl|edition|Foil}}, {{hl|edition|Holographic}}, and {{hl|edition|Polychrome}} cards appear {{hl|orange|2X}} more often Base Hone.png) Hone & ({{hl|edition|Foil}}, {{hl|edition|Holographic}}, and {{hl|edition|Polychrome}} cards appear {{hl|orange|4X}} more often Have at least 5 Joker cards with Foil, Holographic, or Polychrome (or Negative) effect Upgraded Glow Up.png) Glow Up by 2X and 4X respectively.
  • Holographic playing cards are able to be purchased from the Shop if ({{hl|orange|Playing cards}} in shop may have an [[Card modifiers#Enhancements|{{hl|sblue|Enhancement}}]], [[Card modifiers#Edition|{{hl|edition|Edition}}]], and/or a [[Card modifiers#Seal|{{hl|orange|Seal}}]] Buy a total of 20 Playing cards from the shop Upgraded Illusion.png) Illusion is owned.
  • (Add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] effect to {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in hand Available from start. Spectral Aura.png) Aura has a 35% chance to apply Holographic to a selected playing card in your hand.
  • (Select {{hl|orange|2}} cards, convert the {{hl|orange|left}} card into the {{hl|orange|right}} card<br>{{hl|gray|(Drag to rearrange)}} Available from start. Tarot Death.png) Death, (Create {{hl|orange|2}} copies of {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in your hand Available from start. Spectral Cryptid.png) Cryptid, and (If {{hl|orange|first hand}} of round has only {{hl|orange|1}} card, add a permanent copy to deck and draw it to {{hl|orange|hand}} Rare Effect On Played DNA.png) DNA can duplicate Holographic playing cards.

Synergies

[edit | edit source]

Jokers

[edit | edit source]
  • Due to being an edition that is often given to playing cards (through Standard Packs, (Add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] effect to {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in hand Available from start. Spectral Aura.png) Aura, etc.), Jokers that can retrigger playing cards that may have Holographic like (Retrigger {{hl|orange|first}} played card used in scoring {{hl|orange|2}} additional times Beat a Boss Blind with a {{ph|High Card}} Common Retrigger On Scored Hanging Chad.png) Hanging Chad, (Retrigger all played {{hl|orange|face}} cards Play a total of {{hl|orange|300}} face cards Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Sock and Buskin.png) Sock and Buskin, and (Retrigger each played {{hl|orange|2}}, {{hl|orange|3}}, {{hl|orange|4}}, or {{hl|orange|5}} Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Hack.png) Hack have the ability to indirectly provide dozens of Mult through retriggers.
  • Nearly all Jokers that give a large number of Chips, like (This Joker gains {{Chips|+8}} when each played {{hl|orange|2}} is scored <br> {{Currently|{{chips|+0}}}} Win a run in {{hl|orange|18}} or fewer rounds Rare Chips Mixed Wee Joker.png) Wee Joker or (Gains {{Chips|+15}} if played hand contains a {{ph|Straight}} <br> {{Currently|{{chips|+0}}}} Common Chips Mixed Runner.png) Runner, benefit from Mult increasing often much more significantly than Chips.

Decks

[edit | edit source]
  • ( {{Spectral|}} cards may appear in the shop, start with a {{spectral|Hex}} card Win a run with the {{D|Yellow}} on [[Stakes|any difficulty]] Ghost Deck.png) Ghost Deck makes it much easier to find spectral cards like (Add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] effect to {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in hand Available from start. Spectral Aura.png) Aura or (Create {{hl|orange|2}} copies of {{hl|orange|1}} selected card in your hand Available from start. Spectral Cryptid.png) Cryptid that are able to either make existing cards Holographic or create new Holographic cards.

Seals

[edit | edit source]
  • (Retrigger this card {{hl|orange|1}} time Seal Red Seal.png) Red Seals are able to be applied to Holographic playing cards, causing each one to give +20 Mult.

Strategies

[edit | edit source]
  • To maximize Mult, Holographic cards in a hand should be placed to the left of ({{xmult|2}}<br>{{hl|green|1 in 4}} chance to destroy card Enhancement Glass Card.png) Glass cards and other multiplicative Mult sources (like ({{hl|green|1 in 2}} chance for played cards with {{suit|Heart}} suit to give {{xmult|1.5}} when scored Have at least {{hl|orange|30}} cards with {{hl|orange|Heart}} suit in your deck Uncommon Multiplicative Mult On Scored Bloodstone.png) Bloodstone) so that the XMult is applied after the +10 Mult from the Holographic cards.

This game mechanic includes card editions, most of which are directly referenced from Balatro, which is the case for Holographic.

# Material sources

## Joker artwork

The 150 files in `jokers/` and the derived `joker-atlas.png` are original Balatro artwork, credited to LocalThunk / Playstack, obtained from the community [Balatro Wiki Joker collection](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Jokers).

These files are reference artwork for this local recreation/lookdev review. They are not code-drawn original assets and are not represented as assets covered by an open-source license. The Wiki's text license does not establish a redistribution license for the game's artwork.

Exact source URLs, retrieval date, image dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `joker-catalog.json` and `../../research/joker-art-manifest.json`. The Hologram reference is a GIF; its first frame is used here. Wee Joker's source is larger than other cards; its atlas copy is resized with nearest-neighbor sampling.

Names, rarity, prices and English effect descriptions were transcribed from the Wiki. Wiki textual content is offered under [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/), with additional terms where applicable. These research definitions have not been checked against a running original game.

## Other materials

The 65 playing cards, card backs, consumables, booster wrappers and seals in the root of this directory are code-drawn study assets generated by `scripts/generate-assets.py`. The five pixel pouch designs rendered in the UI use 15 original code-drawn sprite layers in `packaging/`, generated by `scripts/generate-packaging.py`, and modest CSS depth in `src/packaging.ts` and `src/packaging.css`.

## Holographic planes

`holograms/` contains derived paper and subject layers for the 215 source materials. They are generated by `scripts/generate-holograms.py` using a connected-background cutout heuristic. These are illustrative 2.5D planes, not 3D models or measured depth maps. The Joker derivatives retain the original game's artwork attribution and copyright. The manifest records the source file and its hash for each pair.

## Glass print layers

`glass/` contains transparent ink layers for the 215 source materials. `scripts/generate-glass.py` removes connected paper colours while retaining printed marks. This is a visual approximation, not semantic segmentation or physical optics. Derived Joker artwork retains the original LocalThunk / Playstack attribution and copyright. The manifest records the source file, its hash and the estimated clear fraction.

## Consumable artwork

The 52 files in `consumables/` are original Balatro card artwork credited to LocalThunk / Playstack, collected from the community [Tarot](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Tarot_cards), [Planet](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Planet_cards) and [Spectral](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Spectral_cards) collections on 2026-11-08. They are local research references, not original study art or assets with an asserted open-source license. Exact source URLs, native 142x190 dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are in `../../research/consumable-art-manifest.json`.

Descriptions are Chinese factual summaries of the Wiki rule tables; the Wiki text attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 terms above apply. Simplified Chinese names were cross-checked against each individual Wiki card's “In other languages” table and the [Balatro HQ Chinese consumable collection](https://balatrohq.com/zh/consumables/). The runtime serves all artwork locally without contacting either site.

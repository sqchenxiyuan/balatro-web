Polychrome
Type
Edition
Effect
X1.5 Mult

Polychrome is an edition card modifier that gives X1.5 Mult when on a playing card or Joker. Polychrome playing cards give X1.5 Mult when played or retriggered, and Polychrome Jokers give X1.5 Mult when Jokers are calculated, (after playing cards are scored.)

Polychrome adds +$5 to the buy price of jokers and playing cards.

Usage notes

[edit | edit source]
  • If Polychrome is on a card that gives additive Mult, the additive Mult will be added first, then the Polychrome will multiply the result by X1.5 Mult (e.g, ({{Mult|+4}} Common Additive Mult Independent Joker.png) Joker would give +4 Mult, then give X1.5 Mult).

Synergies

[edit | edit source]

Seals

[edit | edit source]
  • (Retrigger this card {{hl|orange|1}} time Seal Red Seal.png) Red Seals retrigger playing cards 1 additional time, which gives X2.25 Mult if that card is polychrome.

Jokers

[edit | edit source]
  • (Retrigger {{hl|orange|first}} played card used in scoring {{hl|orange|2}} additional times Beat a Boss Blind with a {{ph|High Card}} Common Retrigger On Scored Hanging Chad.png) Hanging Chad can give X3.375 Mult if your first scored card is Polychrome. While Hanging Chad is best utilized early game when Polychrome cards in your deck are limited, it can still be viable well into endless.
  • (Retrigger all played {{hl|orange|face}} cards Play a total of {{hl|orange|300}} face cards Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Sock and Buskin.png) Sock and Buskin, (Retrigger all played cards in {{hl|orange|final hand}} of the round Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Dusk.png) Dusk, (Retrigger each played {{hl|orange|2}}, {{hl|orange|3}}, {{hl|orange|4}}, or {{hl|orange|5}} Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Hack.png) Hack, and (Retrigger all cards played for the next {{hl|orange|10}} hands Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Seltzer.png) Seltzer can retrigger your played Polychrome cards, essentially doubling Polychrome's effect. While all of these are viable, (Retrigger all played {{hl|orange|face}} cards Play a total of {{hl|orange|300}} face cards Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Sock and Buskin.png) Sock and Buskin and (Retrigger each played {{hl|orange|2}}, {{hl|orange|3}}, {{hl|orange|4}}, or {{hl|orange|5}} Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Hack.png) Hack are the best as they can be utilized multiple times per round and are not consumed.
  • Jokers with exponential scaling potential such as (Played Kings and Queens each give {{xmult|2}} when scored Find this [[Jokers|Joker]] from the [[the Soul|{{hl|sblue|Soul}}]] card. Legendary Multiplicative Mult On Scored Triboulet.png) Triboulet and (Each played [[Card Ranks|{{hl|orange|[rank]}}]] of [[Card Suits|{{hl|orange|[suit]}}]] gives {{xmult|2}} when scored<br><small>Card changes every round</small> In one hand, earn at least {{hl|orange|1,000,000}} chips Uncommon Multiplicative Mult On Scored The Idol.png) The Idol are both ideal Jokers to pair with Polychrome cards, providing a significant amount of extra xMult, especially when retriggered.
  • (First played {{hl|orange|face}} card gives {{xmult|2}} when scored Common Multiplicative Mult On Scored Photograph.png) Photograph is great if you have Polychrome face cards, or have (All cards are considered {{hl|orange|face}} cards Uncommon Effect N/A (Passive) Pareidolia.png) Pareidolia to trigger its effect on a non-face Polychrome card.
  • (After {{hl|orange|2}} rounds, sell this card to {{hl|orange|Duplicate}} a random Joker <br> {{currently|{{hl|orange|0}}/2}} <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] from copy)}} Win a run without ever having more than {{hl|orange|4 Jokers}} Rare Effect N/A (Passive) Invisible Joker.png) Invisible Joker can copy a Polychrome Joker, the new Joker will keep the Polychrome edition.

Consumables

[edit | edit source]
  • (Add [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] to a random {{hl|orange|Joker,}} {{hl|red|-1}} hand size Available from start. Spectral Ectoplasm.png) Ectoplasm allows for an extra Joker to be utilized, and its -1 hand size is not much of a downside if your deck is comprised multiple copies of 1 card. Because Polychrome cards only activate when scored, there is no need to have a large hand size.
  • (Create a copy of a random [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]], destroy all other Jokers <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes {{hl|edition|Negative}} from copy)}} Available from start. Spectral Ankh.png) Ankh can copy a Polychrome Joker. If that Joker is (Can't be sold or destroyed Sticker Eternal Sticker.png) Eternal, then multiple copies of a Polychrome Joker can be made with Ankh.

Strategies

[edit | edit source]
  • Polychrome Jokers score at the same time as other Jokers in your Joker slots, including jokers such as (Played cards with [[Card Ranks|{{hl|orange|even}}]] rank give {{Mult|+4}} when scored <br> {{hl|gray|(10, 8, 6, 4, 2)}} Common Additive Mult On Scored Even Steven.png) Even Steven, who trigger when a card is scored. It's best to move your Polychrome Jokers to the right of any additive Mult Jokers.

In other media

[edit | edit source]
  • In the same update where the Ante Chamber DLC was introduced in Vampire Survivors, a game mechanic that mainly gives the player stat bonuses, the Survarot, was introduced. This game mechanic includes card editions, most of which are directly referenced from Balatro, which is the case for Polychrome.
    • Even Polychrome's effect in Vampire Survivors is similar in vein to Polychrome in Balatro, where both provide multipliers to specific values.

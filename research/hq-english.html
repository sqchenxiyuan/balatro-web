Balatro Card Enhancements

Every way to modify a playing card in Balatro: 8 enhancements, 4 editions and 4 seals. A single card can hold one of each at once. Each entry below shows the exact effect, the Tarot or Spectral card that applies it, and Balatro HQ notes.

Showing 16 of 16 modifiers
BonusB
Enhancement
+30 Chips when this card is scored.
Applied by: The Hierophant (Tarot) — enhances 2 cards
Flat chip filler, cheap to apply two at a time. Flat Chips fall off once you have multiplicative sources.
MultB
Enhancement
+4 Mult when this card is scored.
Applied by: The Empress (Tarot) — enhances 2 cards
Flat Mult. Solid early, outscaled by Glass and X-Mult sources later in a run.
WildA
Enhancement
Counts as every suit at once.
Applied by: The Lovers (Tarot) — enhances 1 card
Fixes Flushes and suit-based jokers without locking your whole deck to one suit.
GlassS
Enhancement
X2 Mult, with a 1 in 4 chance to shatter (be destroyed) after scoring.
Applied by: Justice (Tarot) — enhances 1 card
Highest scoring per card. Pairs with Glass Joker, which scales off every shattered card.
SteelS
Enhancement
X1.5 Mult while this card is held in hand — it does not need to be played.
Applied by: The Chariot (Tarot) — enhances 1 card
Core of Baron and Mime held-in-hand builds. Safe multiplier — never destroyed.
StoneA
Enhancement
+50 Chips. Has no rank or suit and always scores.
Applied by: The Tower (Tarot) — enhances 1 card
Big flat Chips and immune to suit/rank Boss Blinds. Pairs with Stone Joker for scaling.
GoldC
Enhancement
+$3 at end of round if this card is held in hand.
Applied by: The Devil (Tarot) — enhances 1 card
Pure economy, no scoring. Niche outside dedicated money builds.
LuckyC
Enhancement
1 in 5 chance for +20 Mult; 1 in 15 chance for +$20 when scored.
Applied by: The Magician (Tarot) — enhances 2 cards
Swingy. Best with Lucky Cat (scales on each successful trigger) and retriggers.
FoilB
Edition · card + joker
+50 Chips.
Applied by: Aura (Spectral) on a card; shop rolls and the Hone voucher on Jokers
Flat Chips. Fine on a Joker you already want; rarely worth chasing on its own.
HolographicA
Edition · card + joker
+10 Mult.
Applied by: Aura (Spectral) on a card; shop rolls and the Hone voucher on Jokers
Flat +10 Mult is strong on a high-value Joker.
PolychromeS
Edition · card + joker
X1.5 Mult.
Applied by: Aura (Spectral) on a card; shop rolls and the Glow Up voucher on Jokers
Multiplicative — one of the best single modifiers in the game on a strong Joker.
NegativeS
Edition · joker only
On a Joker: takes no Joker slot (effectively +1 slot). On a consumable: +1 consumable slot.
Applied by: Ectoplasm (Spectral) on a Joker; Perkeo can copy consumables as Negative
No scoring effect — its value is the free slot. Negative on a strong Joker is a top-tier find.
Gold SealC
Seal
+$3 when this card is played and scored.
Applied by: Talisman (Spectral)
Economy on every play of the card. Stacks with retriggers.
Red SealS
Seal
Retriggers this card — it scores twice.
Applied by: Deja Vu (Spectral)
Doubles every other modifier on the card. The best seal for scoring.
Blue SealB
Seal
Creates the Planet card for your final played hand, if this card is still held at end of round.
Applied by: Trance (Spectral)
Free hand-leveling. The card must be held, not played, at round end.
Purple SealC
Seal
Creates a Tarot card when this card is discarded.
Applied by: Medium (Spectral)
Consumable generation for discard-heavy decks. Needs free consumable slots.

How modifiers stack

A single playing card can carry one enhancement, one edition and one seal at the same time, and they all fire together. The strongest decks chase cards that hold all three.

Glass + Polychrome + Red Seal
X2 (Glass) then X1.5 (Polychrome), retriggered by the Red Seal. One card swings a hand hard.
Steel + Red Seal
X1.5 while held, and in a Baron/Mime build the Red Seal retriggers the held-card effect.
Stone + Foil
+50 Chips from Stone, +50 from Foil, with no suit for a Boss Blind to punish.

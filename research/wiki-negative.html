Negative is an edition card modifier that gives +1 Joker slot or Consumable slot, depending on what type of card is Negative. Negative consumables can only be acquired through (Creates a [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] copy of {{hl|orange|1}} random [[Consumables|{{hl|orange|consumable}}]] card in your possession at the end of the [[The Shop|{{hl|orange|shop}}]] Find this [[Jokers|Joker]] from the [[the Soul|{{hl|sblue|Soul}}]] card. Legendary Effect N/A (Passive) Perkeo.png) Perkeo. The Negative edition is the rarest edition in Balatro, having a 0.3% chance to be applied to a Joker in the shop. However, the Negative edition is also the most powerful edition in the game, as the ability to hold an additional Joker has significantly more potential than even ({{xmult|1.5}} Edition Joker.png) Polychrome. The Negative edition inverts the colors of whatever it is applied to, and adds an additional shine on top.

Negative adds +$5 to the buy price of jokers and consumables.

Acquisition

[edit | edit source]

There are a few ways of acquiring a Joker with a Negative edition:

  • Jokers in the shop can naturally appear with the Negative edition, with a 0.3% chance of this happening.
  • (Add [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] to a random {{hl|orange|Joker,}} {{hl|red|-1}} hand size Available from start. Spectral Ectoplasm.png) Ectoplasm applies the Negative edition to a random Joker in the player's Joker slots at the cost of -1 hand size.
  • ( Discover the {{edition|Negative}} edition Negative tag.png) Negative Tag guarantees that the next Joker (without any other edition) in the shop has the Negative edition.

As for Negative consumables, the only way to acquire them is with (Creates a [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] copy of {{hl|orange|1}} random [[Consumables|{{hl|orange|consumable}}]] card in your possession at the end of the [[The Shop|{{hl|orange|shop}}]] Find this [[Jokers|Joker]] from the [[the Soul|{{hl|sblue|Soul}}]] card. Legendary Effect N/A (Passive) Perkeo.png) Perkeo. Perkeo creates Negative copy of a random consumable card, and can do so to any consumable except (Upgrade every [[Poker Hands|{{hl|purple|poker hand}}]] by {{hl|orange|1}} level Available from start. Spectral Black Hole.png) Black Hole and (Creates a [[Legendary Jokers|{{hl|purple|Legendary}}]] Joker <br> {{room}} Available from start. Spectral The Soul.png) The Soul, as these 2 consumables cannot be added to your consumable slots. Because Perkeo is a Legendary Joker, however, having Negative consumables is incredibly rare, more so than Negative Jokers.

Usage notes

[edit | edit source]
  • (After {{hl|orange|2}} rounds, sell this card to {{hl|orange|Duplicate}} a random Joker <br> {{currently|{{hl|orange|0}}/2}} <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] from copy)}} Win a run without ever having more than {{hl|orange|4 Jokers}} Rare Effect N/A (Passive) Invisible Joker.png) Invisible Joker and (Create a copy of a random [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]], destroy all other Jokers <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes {{hl|edition|Negative}} from copy)}} Available from start. Spectral Ankh.png) Ankh will remove the Negative edition from a Joker it copies, if it has it. This is the only way to remove an edition from a card in Balatro.
  • If a Negative Joker appears in the Shop, it can be purchased even if there are no extra Joker slots available, unless, in rare cases, the current Jokers exceed the Joker slots (for example, selling Negative (After {{hl|orange|2}} rounds, sell this card to {{hl|orange|Duplicate}} a random Joker <br> {{currently|{{hl|orange|0}}/2}} <br> {{hl|gray|(Removes [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] from copy)}} Win a run without ever having more than {{hl|orange|4 Jokers}} Rare Effect N/A (Passive) Invisible Joker.png) Invisible Joker to replicate a Joker or in the ( When ante {{hl|orange|4}} boss is defeated:<br>- all Jokers become {{hl|orange|eternal}}<br>- set Joker slots to {{hl|orange|0}} Banned [[Blinds]]: {{Blind|Verdant Leaf}}) Typecast Challenge).
  • Unlike other modifiers, the Negative effect of Joker remains effective even if it gets debuffed; only by selling Negative Joker will the Joker slot decrease.
    • However, if a debuffed Joker (either from ( Showdown Crimson Heart.webp) Crimson Heart or (Debuffed after {{hl|orange|5}} rounds <br> {{hl|gray|({{hl|orange|5}} remaining)}} Sticker Perishable Sticker.png) Perishable sticker) gains () Negative enhancement afterwards, the Joker slot will not increase, and selling it will still decrease Joker slot, resulting in permanent Joker slot loss, which is likely a bug.

Strategies

[edit | edit source]
  • The Negative edition is almost always worth taking, especially if a good Joker appears in the shop with the Negative edition. Even if the Joker is not helpful for your build, it can be worth picking up anyways as it increases the money earned from (Gives the total sell value of all current [[Jokers]]<br>{{hl|grey|(Max of}} {{hl|orange|{{Money|50}}}}{{hl|grey|)}} Available from start. Tarot Temperance.png) Temperance and can completely nullify the ( Showdown Verdant Leaf.webp) Verdant Leaf Boss Blind.

Trivia

[edit | edit source]
  • The ({{hl|edition|+1}} Joker Slot Redeem Blank 10 total times Upgraded Antimatter.png) Antimatter voucher always has the Negative filter applied to it when it appears in the shop.
  • While Negative playing cards can be seen in promotional screenshots, they have never been used in public versions of the game.[1]
  • In versions before 0.9.0q-DEMO, the shader used for Negative appeared differently than it has since.
    • Remnants can be seen in certain screenshots on Balatro's Steam Page, as well as early gameplay footage and reviews.

In other media

[edit | edit source]
  • In the same update where the Ante Chamber DLC was introduced in Vampire Survivors, a game mechanic that mainly gives the player stat bonuses, the Survarot, was introduced. This game mechanic includes card editions, most of which are directly referenced from Balatro. This is the case for this edition as well, though it is called "Inverted" in Vampire Survivors.
